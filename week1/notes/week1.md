# Notes – Lessons Learned 

## Key Learnings
- Creativity is important: choosing what to represent in queries affects clarity and insight.
- Time management is crucial: complex queries and joins take longer than expected.
- Some SQL functions are similar but behave differently:
  - `RANK()` vs `ROW_NUMBER()` – both assign order but handle ties differently.
  - `INNER JOIN` vs `LEFT JOIN` – choosing the right join affects data completeness.
- Planning queries before writing saves debugging time.
